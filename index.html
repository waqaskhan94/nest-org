FAQ

Questions Parents Often Ask

Everything you need to know before enrolling your child at NEST.

What ages do you accept? ▾

We accept children between ages 2 to 20. Early intervention starts from age 2 and transition programs go up to 20 years.

Is the first assessment really free? ▾

Yes! We offer a complimentary initial assessment for all new families to understand your child's needs and recommend the right programs.

What conditions do you support? ▾

Autism, ADHD, Down Syndrome, Cerebral Palsy, speech delays, learning disabilities, sensory processing disorder, and other developmental differences.

How do I enroll my child? ▾

Contact us to schedule a free assessment. After evaluation our team will suggest a personalized program. Enrollment is completed within one week.

Do you provide transport? ▾

We do not currently offer transport services. Parents are requested to arrange their own conveyance to our center at DHA Phase 2, Sector G, Islamabad.

Can parents watch therapy sessions? ▾

Yes! Parents are welcome to observe sessions. We actively involve families through parent training programs and home activity guides.
