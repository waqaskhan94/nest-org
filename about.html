About Us

Who We Are

Special Needs Education & Therapy Center in Islamabad

NEST was founded with one belief, every child with special needs deserves world-class care, close to home.

Our Story

Born From a Parent's Heart

NEST was founded by parents who experienced firsthand the challenges of finding quality, compassionate care for children with special needs in Pakistan. What began as a small therapy room has grown into a full-service school and therapy center.

Today, NEST serves hundreds of families across Pakistan from our center in DHA Phase 2, Sector G, Islamabad, providing hope, progress, and belonging to every child who walks through our doors.

"We don't just run a school and therapy center, we build futures, one child at a time."
NEST Founders

How We Work

Our Holistic Approach

We don't just offer therapy, we build a complete ecosystem of support around each child and family. Here's our proven five-step process that delivers real, lasting results.

1
Comprehensive Assessment
A multidisciplinary evaluation covering cognitive, communication, motor, and social-emotional domains to build a complete picture of your child's strengths and needs.
2
Personalized Therapy Plan
Our team designs an individualized plan with clear, measurable goals aligned to your child's specific profile, reviewed and updated every three months.
3
Expert Therapy Sessions
Engaging, evidence-based one-on-one and small-group sessions delivered by our certified specialists in a warm, stimulating environment.
4
Family Training & Support
We train parents and caregivers with practical home strategies so therapy goals are reinforced throughout the day, not just during clinic hours.
5
Ongoing Progress Tracking
Regular progress reports, parent meetings, and goal reviews ensure transparency and keep the entire team, including your family, aligned at all times.
