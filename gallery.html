Inside Our Islamabad Special Education & Therapy Center

Every photo above is taken inside NEST's purpose-built facility in DHA Phase 2, Sector G, Islamabad, a calm, sensory-friendly space designed specifically for children with autism, ADHD, Down syndrome, speech delays, and other developmental needs. Our classrooms use low-stimulation colours, flexible seating, and structured visual routines that help children with special needs feel safe, focused, and ready to learn.

Alongside the learning areas, our dedicated therapy rooms host one-on-one ABA, speech, occupational, and physical therapy sessions, while our activity and sensory zones support play-based learning, motor-skill development, and social interaction. From everyday classroom moments to milestone celebrations and family events, these images reflect the warm, expert-led environment our team creates each day, the same care we invite every Islamabad family to experience in person.
