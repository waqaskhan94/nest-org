What Is Sensory Integration Therapy?

Sensory integration therapy helps children whose brains have trouble organizing and responding to everyday sensory input, touch, sound, movement, textures, light and smell. Some children are over-responsive and become overwhelmed by things that seem ordinary to others, such as tags in clothing, loud rooms or being touched unexpectedly. Others are under-responsive and seek out intense movement, crashing, spinning or deep pressure to feel regulated. At N.E.S.T, sensory integration therapy is most often delivered within our occupational therapy program for children with autism, ADHD, sensory processing differences or anxiety around certain textures, sounds or crowded spaces, using purposeful, play-based activities that help the nervous system respond more comfortably.

Who It's For & What a Session Looks Like

Families in Islamabad often come to N.E.S.T when a child covers their ears at normal sounds, avoids or constantly seeks out touch, melts down in crowded or noisy places, struggles with certain clothing or food textures, seems unusually clumsy, or craves constant spinning, jumping and crashing. Sensory integration therapy also supports children with autism, ADHD and anxiety whose sensory regulation makes it harder to sit still, focus or cope with change. A sensory assessment helps us build a plan around your child's specific sensory profile.

Our Approach & What a Session Looks Like

Sessions usually last 45-60 minutes and take place in a dedicated sensory gym using swings, crash mats, textured play materials, brushing protocols and purposeful movement activities chosen for your child. It looks like play, but every activity is selected to help your child's nervous system respond more calmly to touch, sound and movement over time. Parents are coached to continue simple regulation strategies at home, and we share regular progress updates.

How Sensory Integration Fits Into Your Child's Wider Plan

Sensory regulation underlies a child's ability to engage with almost everything else, sitting in a classroom, following instructions, learning to speak, and managing behavior. That is why sensory integration therapy at N.E.S.T is never delivered in isolation. Our occupational therapists coordinate closely with our special educators, ABA therapists and speech-language pathologists, so a child who is overwhelmed by noise or touch gets support regulated before (and during) their academic, behavioral and communication work, rather than fighting through sensory overload in every session. Families usually see sensory strategies carried across their child's whole day at N.E.S.T, not just inside the sensory gym.

Sensory Integration Therapy FAQs

Answers to common questions from Islamabad families about sensory integration therapy at N.E.S.T.

What is sensory integration therapy?▾
Sensory integration therapy is a structured, play-based approach that helps a child's nervous system process and respond to sensory input such as touch, sound, movement and textures more comfortably. At N.E.S.T in Islamabad, sensory integration techniques help children who are over- or under-responsive to sensory input feel calmer, more focused and better able to take part in daily life.
What are signs my child needs sensory integration therapy?▾
Common signs include covering ears at normal sounds, avoiding or constantly seeking out touch, becoming distressed by certain clothing or food textures, struggling in crowded or noisy places, seeming unusually clumsy or craving spinning and jumping, and meltdowns that seem out of proportion to the situation. A sensory assessment at N.E.S.T can confirm whether sensory integration therapy will help.
Is sensory integration therapy the same as occupational therapy?▾
Not exactly. Sensory integration therapy is a specific therapeutic approach, most often delivered by occupational therapists as part of a child's broader occupational therapy plan. At N.E.S.T, our occupational therapists use sensory integration techniques, sensory-gym equipment and play-based regulation activities within OT sessions, so families typically access sensory integration therapy through our occupational therapy program rather than as a completely separate service.
What happens in a sensory integration therapy session?▾
Sessions usually last 45-60 minutes and take place in a sensory gym, using swings, crash mats, textured play materials, brushing protocols and purposeful movement activities chosen for your child's sensory profile. Sessions look like play, but each activity is selected to help your child's nervous system respond more calmly to input. Parents are coached on strategies to continue at home.
Do you serve families from Rawalpindi looking for sensory integration therapy?▾
Yes. While N.E.S.T is based in DHA Phase 2, Sector G, Islamabad, many families travel from Rawalpindi and nearby areas for sensory integration therapy, since our team works alongside special educators, ABA therapists and speech-language pathologists under one roof.
Is sensory integration therapy available online?▾
Sensory integration therapy itself requires hands-on equipment and is delivered in person, but our Clinical Director offers online consultations and parent coaching over video call for families who need guidance on sensory strategies between visits or cannot attend in person, including those outside Islamabad or abroad.

How much does sensory integration therapy cost in Islamabad?▾
Fees depend on how many sessions per week your child's sensory plan needs and whether it's combined with other services like occupational therapy. We start every family with a sensory assessment so we can recommend the right plan and give you a clear, no-obligation quote before you commit to anything.
How long until we see results from sensory integration therapy?▾
Every child's nervous system responds at its own pace, but many families in Islamabad notice better tolerance of sounds, textures or crowded spaces within 4-8 weeks of consistent sessions. We share regular progress updates so you can see exactly how your child is responding.
