Therapy FAQs

Everything you need to know about our therapy services at N.E.S.T.

How do I know which therapy my child needs? ▾

We offer a free comprehensive assessment where our team of certified specialists evaluates your child's development across communication, behavior, motor skills, and emotional wellbeing. Based on the results, we recommend a tailored therapy plan — no guesswork, just expert guidance.

How long does each therapy session last? ▾

Sessions typically last 45–60 minutes depending on the therapy type and your child's age and needs. Our therapists design each session to maximize engagement and measurable progress within that window.

Are your therapists certified and experienced? ▾

Yes! All our therapists are nationally certified and have extensive hands-on experience working with children with autism, ADHD, Down Syndrome, cerebral palsy, and other developmental conditions. They undergo regular professional training to stay current with best practices.

Can parents observe therapy sessions? ▾

Absolutely! We encourage parent involvement and full transparency. Parents are welcome to observe sessions, and our therapists provide regular progress updates. We also offer dedicated parent training programs to extend therapy benefits at home.

How soon will we see progress? ▾

Progress varies by child and therapy type, but many families notice improvements within the first few weeks. Our team sets clear measurable goals and tracks progress consistently so you always know how your child is advancing.

Can my child receive multiple therapies simultaneously? ▾

Yes! Many children benefit greatly from a combination of therapies. Our multidisciplinary team coordinates care across speech, occupational, ABA, and physical therapy to ensure each child receives a holistic and well-integrated treatment plan.
